
# Build Your Festival — Eco Minigame System

An interactive educational mobile application developed for **De Ontdekfabriek**, designed to teach young people about sustainability through festival-themed mini-games.

**Project Status:** Completed  
**Project Type:** Group Project — External Client

## Project Overview

Build Your Festival is an educational application that combines interactive gameplay with sustainability awareness.

Designed for young users around the age of 14, the application allows players to explore different areas of a festival and discover how their decisions affect the environment.

The project combines digital mini-games with a physical festival board through QR codes, creating an experience that connects physical and digital interaction.

Our team was responsible for redesigning and improving the existing application to make the games more engaging, interactive and educational.

## Key Features

### Festival-Themed Mini-Games

The application includes five themed mini-games:

- **Hangout:** Explore sustainability decisions related to festival relaxation areas.
- **Food:** Learn about sustainable choices involving festival food.
- **Toilet:** Explore environmental considerations related to festival facilities.
- **Trash/Waste:** Learn about waste management and recycling.
- **Stage:** Configure festival stage equipment while considering sustainability.

### Eco Scoring System

- Player decisions influence sustainability scores.
- The scoring system encourages environmentally responsible choices.
- Players receive feedback about the consequences of their decisions.

### Interactive Gameplay

- Role-based gameplay across different festival areas.
- Visual and audio feedback.
- Animated elements that respond to player interactions.
- QR code integration with a physical festival board.

My primary responsibility was developing and improving the visual and interactive experience of the **Stage Game**.

- Conducted research into stage technology to improve the realism of the game.
- Participated in competitor analysis to investigate engaging gameplay mechanics.

- Collaborated on further improvements to the game concept and user experience.

### 2. Stage Visual Development

- Implemented the visual representation of the festival stage.
- Developed stage elements that reflected player equipment choices.
- Created and integrated assets including stage equipment, LED lights, drones and fireworks.
- Redesigned the stage environment to create a more engaging gameplay experience.
- Improved the stage perspective and asset visibility based on client feedback.

### 3. Animations & Interactive Feedback

- Implemented animated drones moving across the stage.
